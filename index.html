Solution · Safety & Security

Make glass safer.

Designed to make glass safer, both by strengthening it against breakage and helping hold dangerous shards of shattered glass in situations where breakage does occur.

Start a project →Browse suitable films →

Who this helps

Architects, main contractors, building control officers, and facilities managers responsible for glazing impact safety compliance (Regulation 14), duty of care, and blast risk mitigation.

How film helps

The multi-layer optical film strengthens existing clear float or toughened glazing against premature failure, absorbing impact energy and containing fractured fragments safely in the perimeter frame.

Frequently asked

Does film upgrade non-safety glass to UK Building Regulations?Yes, Opalux safety films are tested to BS EN 12600 (Class 1B1 / 2B2) satisfying Workplace Regulation 14 and Approved Document K.
Can films be installed without replacing existing frames?Yes, films are retrofitted directly to the existing glass in situ with minimal disruption to building occupants.
Is edge retention anchoring required?For standard human impact, standard wet-edge trimming is sufficient. For blast mitigation, structural silicone or mechanical battening is recommended.

Frequently asked questions

Will window film damage my double glazing?

No. We carry out a thermal stress assessment before recommending any film for double-glazed units. Our films are specifically selected for compatibility with sealed units and come with a manufacturer's warranty covering thermal breakage.

How long does installation take?

A typical home takes half a day to a full day depending on the number of windows. Large conservatories may take a full day. The film is ready to clean and use normally within 30 days of fitting.

Can I still see out of my windows?

Absolutely. Our solar and privacy films let you see out clearly while reducing heat and blocking the view in during daylight. Only frosted or whiteout films obscure the outward view, and those are chosen specifically for bathrooms and ground-floor privacy.

Does film work on conservatory roofs?

Yes. We install specialist roof films on polycarbonate and glass conservatory roofs to reject heat from above — the main source of overheating in most conservatories.

What is the warranty?

All Opalux residential films carry a 10-year limited manufacturer's warranty covering peeling, bubbling, discolouration and delamination. Our safety films also carry EN 12600 impact certification.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the film type, glass area and access. We provide free, no-obligation quotes after surveying your home. Most rooms cost less than replacing a single window.
